■展覧会名：　Contemporary Art Salon, Spring 2019　 (春季當代藝術沙龍展）


■会期　　：　2019年 4月25日　－　4月28日


■場所　　：　台北オークションセンター　


MF., No. 5, Sec. 5, Xinyi Rd., Xinyi Dist., Taipei City 11011, Taiwan (R.O.C.)


■　現代アートサロンは台北国際会議センターの５階にある台北オークションセンターで年に４回開催されるイベントです。


その中で今回は初の試みであり、世界中から選抜された若手アーティスト１００名による展示・即売会を行います。


コレクターズ・パーティー　　４月２５日（木）18:00-22:00


オープニングVIPパーティー　　４月２６日（金）17:00-22:00


一般来場　　　　　　　　　　４月２７日（土）、２８日（日）　11:00-19:00　（入場料　100台湾ドル）


Auction Center Taipei (ACT) is pleased to announce to launch its first Contemporary Art Salon which will occur from 25th to 28th of April 2019. Four Contemporary Art Salons are scheduled within the year by ACT. Almost 100 prestigious and promising artists will be selected during this spring show alone. Artists are welcomed to register now in order to participate this remarkable event.


Taipei recently has become the newest cultural capital in Asia and art scenery is blooming thanks to the contribution of TAIPEI DANGDAI and other indicative exhibitions. This has made Taipei one of the enthusiasm cities in Asia to develop a contemporary art scene and ACT is thrilled to start up with Contemporary Art Salon 2019 Spring.


Contemporary Art Salon is just a beginning, in the upcoming future, ACT will provide more services to international artists such as exhibition space rental, artists commissioned sales, brand authorizing, curating for artists, auction …etc. aiming to build up the platform and the win-win situation between the artists and the collectors.


Exhibition venue & schedule:


-Venue: Taipei Auction Center : Floor M, No.5, Xinyi Rd., Xinyi Dist., Taipei City 11011, Taiwan (TWTC Exhibition Hall 1)


-Collectors’ Night : 2019.4.25（Thursday）18:00~22:00(Collectors Only)


-Opening VIP Party : 2019.4.26（Friday）17:00~ 22:00（Invitation Only）



-Open to Public : 2019.4.27（Saturday）- 2019.4.28（Sunday） 11:00~19:00（Ticket TWD 100）
